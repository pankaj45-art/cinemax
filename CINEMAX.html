THEATRE POWER 5× (100% = 5× bass + wide surround)0%
The big one. At 100%: bass 5 times stronger (+14 dB, 5x amplitude), much wider surround, cinema-hall room and low-volume correction, with a smooth bass limiter to avoid buzz. Start at 50% and go up. Use the A/B button (or Space) to compare with the original. No headphones? Pick your speaker type in the profile list (or press Speaker MAX): deep bass is rebuilt as harmonics your speaker can actually play, and the stereo image is widened.
Listening level (how quiet)0%
Set higher the lower your Windows volume is. Restores the bass and treble the ear loses at quiet levels (equal-loudness contour) without raising the overall level, and eases loud passages down smoothly before the limiter.
Virtual bass (deep-bass feel)0%
Adds smooth upper harmonics of the deep bass so it is felt on headphones, laptops and speakers that cannot reproduce it. If you hear any buzz, lower this to 0.
Speaker lowest note (Hz)Auto
How low your speakers can really play. Auto uses the speaker profile (laptop about 190 Hz). Small laptop speakers often start around 200-300 Hz: if the bass sounds thin, move this up toward 250 Hz so the rebuilt bass lands where your speakers are loud enough.
Speaker bass strength (laptop / desktop speakers)50%
Only used when the profile is a speaker (laptop, desktop, TV, soundbar, generic). Sets how strongly deep bass is rebuilt as harmonics your speaker can play. 50% is the tuned default. Raise it if bass is still thin, lower it if you hear buzz.
Theatre room (early reflections)0%
Adds the short wall reflections of a cinema hall for a bigger, in-the-room feel. Strongest on headphones. If sound seems hollow, lower it.
Low-volume compensation15%
Adds a small bass tilt for quiet listening; it does not raise the system volume.
Ambience (delayed depth)0%
If sound seems hollow or doubled, set this to 0 and choose Stereo. Browser capture and device latency are separate from this setting.
Dialogue clarity35%
Dynamic range optimisation90%
Higher values use less compression; Late-night mode adds more levelling.
Output level0 dB
